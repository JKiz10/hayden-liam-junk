Salt Air

The Air Down Here Eats Everything

Live a few blocks from the water long enough and you stop being surprised. Hinges seize. Patio furniture flakes down to bare frame. A bed frame in the shed goes orange. Shelving legs give out. An appliance sitting on a covered porch quietly turns into a shell.

None of it is worth keeping and all of it is a nuisance to move, because corroded metal is heavy, awkward, and sharp in ways that surprise people. Leave it be and let us handle it. We bring gloves and a trailer, and we would rather load it ourselves than hear about somebody slicing a forearm open on a rusted edge.

Appliance shells, patio sets, frames, gate hardware, mowers seized solid, and whatever metal has been leaning against the fence for a few seasons all come with us in one go.

Gear & Dock Material

Traps, Rods, Rope, and the Rest of the Shed

Fishing is not a hobby people around here do occasionally, it is part of the furniture, and it leaves a residue. Every waterfront shed and carport we open has a version of the same collection in it. Cracked coolers. Rods with no guides left. Crab traps flattened by something heavier. Coils of rope stiff with salt. Buckets, chairs, a busted cast net, an outboard cowling with no outboard attached.

All of that loads fine and we take it. Pulled dock boards and old pier lumber too, once they are up and out of the water and stacked where a trailer can pull alongside.

Boats and boat trailers are a separate conversation, and worth an honest one. Size matters and so does the title. Call us first with what you have got and we will tell you plainly whether it is something we can take or something you need a different outfit for.

How We Work

Nothing Leaves This Property Uncovered

Wind comes off the bay with nothing much in its way, and a loose load turns into somebody else's yard problem within a block. So the tarp goes on and gets tied down before we move, every single time, whether the trailer is stacked high or half full.

The same thinking covers the rest of how we work. We walk the area once more before pulling out to catch what shook loose during loading. We do not leave a scattering of foam and paper across your grass as a parting gift.

Access here varies a lot, from a wide-open lot to a narrow shell drive with a boat already parked in it. Mention what we are dealing with when you call and we will sort the approach out ahead of time rather than in front of your house.

Whole Properties

When the Whole Place Has to Be Cleared

Housing down here runs the whole range, from small older homes that have been in one family for decades to places right on the water, and a fair number of them have quietly accumulated for years. Then a sale, an inheritance, a tenant change, or an insurance requirement puts a date on the calendar.

Those jobs get worked in passes. Outside first, usually, because clearing the yard and the driveway makes everything after it easier and gives you something visible to show for day one. Then the sheds and the carport, then room by room inside.

We empty structures completely, down to the bare floor. What we do not do is take structures apart, and that includes piers and decks. Our insurance covers hauling, not demolition. Get someone else to bring it down and we will clear every piece of it off the lot.

Bay Side Questions

What Folks Down Here Ask Us

Do you cover both Bacliff and San Leon, or just one of them?

Both, and we treat them as one run because that is how the geography works. Neither one is a city with its own hall and its own rules, so there is nothing to sort out on that front. Give us the address at 832-425-7749 and we will book it the same way we would book anywhere else on our map.

Will you haul off rusted metal and things that have corroded through?

Yes, and around here that is a large share of what we load. Bed frames, shelving, patio sets that flaked apart, appliance shells, gate frames, wheelbarrows with no bottom left. Salt air finishes off metal faster than most people expect, and we would rather carry it out than watch you cut a hand on it.

Can you take old fishing gear, traps, and dock material?

Gear and material, yes. Rods, nets, crab traps, coolers, buckets, rope, pulled dock boards, and the pile of odds and ends that lives in every waterfront shed. Boats and boat trailers are a separate conversation, because size and title both matter, so call us first on those and we will tell you honestly whether it is a job we can take.

The property has years of accumulation on it. Is that too big a job?

It is usually the kind we like. Long-accumulated lots get worked in passes rather than all at once, and we plan them around whatever your actual deadline is. Send a video walking the property to 832-425-7749 and we will tell you what it realistically takes before you commit to anything.

It gets windy down here. Do you cover the load?

Every time, before we leave the property. A tarp goes over the trailer and gets secured, which keeps light material out of your neighbor's yard and off the road behind us. On the bay side that is not optional, it is just how the job gets done properly.

Are you actually local, or driving down from Houston?

Local. We are a Galveston County outfit, and Bacliff and San Leon are neighbors rather than a long haul at the edge of a service map. That is why you get a person on the phone instead of a call center, and why the answer about scheduling is a real one rather than a maybe.
