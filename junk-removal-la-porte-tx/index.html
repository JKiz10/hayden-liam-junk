After the Wind Quits

Cleanup Season on the Water

When weather comes up the bay, La Porte feels it before the inland towns do. There is not much between open water and a front yard, so limbs snap, fence panels go over in runs rather than one at a time, and whatever was loose on the patio ends up two houses down.

The cleanup part is not complicated, it is just heavy and it never happens in one neat pile. We work a storm yard in passes. Big cuts first, then the fence sections, then the brush and fronds raked out of the beds, and back through until the yard actually reads as clean instead of mostly clean.

One practical favor: pull everything toward a single stack near the drive if you are able. It is the difference between a quick load and a long one, and a quick load is easier on your quote too. If the work is going to stretch out over a week or two, a roll-off in the driveway can make more sense than repeat pickups. We will tell you honestly which one fits.

Garages, Carports & Old Gear

The Boat Is Long Gone. The Gear Is Not.

Every water town has this house. The boat sold years back, and the carport still holds everything that used to ride in it. Coolers with cracked lids, a milk crate of tangled line, life jackets gone stiff, rod holders, a trolling motor that quit and never got looked at again. None of it is worth keeping and none of it ever quite reaches the curb.

Older La Porte homes hold a lot besides. Long-owned houses stack up in the attic, the carport, and along the back fence, and the people who filled them are not always the people who have to empty them now. If you are working through a parent’s place or getting a family house ready to sell, we can take an estate cleanout at whatever pace you need, room by room or all at once.

La Porte Questions

Things People Here Ask First

Can you clear storm debris out of a La Porte yard?

Yes, and it is a big part of why people out here save our number. Cut limbs, snapped trunk sections, palm fronds, fence panels the wind laid over, and whatever else ended up in the flower beds. Rake it toward one spot if you can, since a single pile loads far faster than five scattered ones. If your street is still a mess and access is limited, say so when you call and we will plan around it.

We pulled some old dock and pier boards. Will you take that lumber?

Bring it up where a trailer can reach, tell us roughly how much there is and what kind of lumber it is, and we will give you a straight answer before you count on us. The one hard line is that it has to already be off the structure. We are insured to haul, not to pull apart anything still standing, so the demo half belongs to you or your contractor.

Do you run out to Shoreacres and Morgan’s Point too?

We do. Those little bayside pockets sit right against La Porte and we treat the whole stretch as one run. If you are on a short street off the bay side, mention it when you call so we know what we are pulling into before we get there.

Our house is older and the driveway is narrow. Is that a problem?

Not usually, but details help. Tell us how wide the drive is, whether there is a low carport, if the fence gate is standard width, and whether the ground beside the concrete stays soft. We would rather park at the curb and carry a little farther than crack a driveway edge or leave ruts in a yard.

Water got into a room. Can you take the wet carpet and drywall out?

Yes. Once the pieces are cut out and pulled, we load them. Soaked carpet and pad get heavy fast, so what looks like a small pile is often more weight than people expect. That is normal for us. Tell us how many rooms and we will bring the right setup instead of guessing.

How does a quote work for a La Porte haul?

Free, and yours to walk away from. Call 832-425-7749 or text a photo of the pile and we will price it. We look at what is actually there instead of running a stopwatch, so you know the number before anybody lifts anything.
