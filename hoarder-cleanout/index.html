Questions

Hoarder Clean Out Questions

How much does a hoarder clean out cost?

Every home is different, so we quote per job. Text us photos or have us come walk the home, and you get one written price before anything is loaded. That is the price. The quote is free, and big jobs are never billed by the hour.

Do I have to sort or bag anything first?

No. That is our job. Tell us what you want to keep and we write it down before anything moves. Papers, photos, cash and jewelry get set aside for you as we go, even if they were not on the list. If we are not sure about something, we ask before it goes.

Will the neighbors know?

Not from us. We set the start time with you, and our crew does not talk about the job while we are at the home. Your privacy is part of the job.

Can I set this up for my mom, dad or another family member?

Yes. We just need a yes from the person with legal say over the home, like the owner, an executor or someone with power of attorney. We check that before we schedule. You do not have to be there while we work, as long as the keep list is set and we can get in. If someone still lives in the home, they set the pace.

Do you handle biohazards or sanitize the house?

No. We remove the contents and haul them away. We do not do biohazard work, meaning human or animal waste, sharps in volume, blood or heavy active mold, and we do not sanitize, disinfect or deep clean. If the home needs any of that, a qualified remediation company should go first. Tell us what you see and we will point you to one. Once that work is done, we can clear the rest.
