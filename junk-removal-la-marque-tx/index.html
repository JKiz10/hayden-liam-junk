Garages & Back Sheds

The Garage Nobody Has Parked In Since the Nineties

You know the one. A ladder against the wall, a stack of folding chairs, paint cans that dried solid a decade ago, three rolls of carpet padding, and a lawn mower that has not turned over in two summers. It happened one box at a time, which is why it never got dealt with all at once.

We deal with it all at once. Two people, a trailer at the apron, and a couple of hours of steady carrying. You stand there and say keep or go. That is the entire process, and by the end you can see the back wall again.

Back sheds count too. If the door has swollen shut and the roof leaks over a pile of who-knows-what, we will empty it down to the floor. One thing to be straight about, though: we clear buildings out, we do not tear buildings down.

Estates & Downsizing

When a Family Home Finally Has to Be Emptied

This is quiet work and we treat it that way. Sometimes a parent has moved into care, sometimes there is a probate deadline, sometimes a house simply has to be listed and the family lives three states away. However it came about, somebody is standing in a hallway wondering where to even begin.

Start by telling us what stays. Photo albums, the china, a chest of drawers somebody wants, whatever it is. Set it aside or point it out, and we treat every other thing in the house as ours to carry. Closets, the attic hatch, under the beds, the utility room, the garage.

We work at whatever pace suits you. Some families want it emptied in a single day so it is over with. Others want the garage and shed cleared first and the inside handled later, once the sorting is done. Both are fine, and we will come back.

What People Here Ask Before They Book

My mother's house in La Marque has to be cleared before it sells. Can you take the whole thing?

Yes, and it is a lot of what we do here. Room by room, closets, the attic hatch, the utility room, the garage, and the shed out back if there is one. You mark what stays and we treat everything else as ours to carry. Nobody in the family has to lift a box. Call or text 832-425-7749 and we will walk the house with you before we quote a number.

Will you get the old fridge and the deep freeze out of the garage?

We will. Appliances are two-man items and that is exactly why people call instead of borrowing a truck. Washers, dryers, ranges, a garage refrigerator, a chest freezer nobody has opened in years, water heaters. Leave them where they sit. We bring the straps and the dolly.

Can we clear the house in stages instead of all at once?

Absolutely, and families often do. Some folks want the garage and the shed gone first so they can see progress, then take the inside slower once the sorting is done. Book us for the first pass, decide afterward, and we will come back for the rest whenever you are ready.

Our street is narrow and there is no alley behind the house. Does that stop you?

No. Plenty of the older La Marque blocks are exactly like that, and we plan the park before we pull up. Tell us if the driveway is short, if there is a car that cannot move, if the ditch line is soft, or if a neighbor parks close. Knowing beforehand is all it takes.
