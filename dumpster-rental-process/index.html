Step by Step

Four Simple Steps

From your first call to the empty driveway, here is exactly how it goes.

1. Reserve It

Call or text 832-425-7749. Tell us what you are working on, where the dumpster is going, and when you want it. We help you pick the 14-yard or the 20-yard, lock in a drop-off day, usually same or next day, and give you the flat price up front. No account, no deposit games.

2. We Deliver It

On your drop-off day we bring your roll-off on our trailer and set it exactly where you want it. We put boards down to protect your driveway and line it up so it is easy to load. You do not even need to be home, just tell us where to place it.

3. You Fill It

Load it over your rental at your own pace, no one rushing you. Fill it right up to the top, just keep everything level with the rim so it is safe to haul, nothing heaped over the sides.

4. We Haul It Away

Give us a call when you are done, or when your rental is up, and we come back, pick it up, and take everything to the proper facility. You never touch the dump. Need a few more days? Extra days are $100 each.

Before You Load It

What Can't Go In the Dumpster

A handful of things are banned from Texas landfills by law or just are not safe to haul. Not sure about something? Text a photo to 832-425-7749 and we will tell you straight.

Hazardous & Chemical

Wet paint, stains and solvents, pesticides and weed killer, pool chemicals, and cleaners like bleach or ammonia.

Automotive

Motor oil, gas, diesel and antifreeze, oil filters, and propane or compressed-gas tanks.

Tires & Batteries

Whole tires, car (lead-acid) batteries, and lithium or rechargeable batteries. All are banned from Texas landfills and need a recycler.

Freon Appliances

Fridges, freezers, and AC units still holding Freon. We can still haul these for you separately with our junk removal, just ask.

Electronics

TVs, computer monitors, and other e-waste. Texas treats these as recyclables, not landfill trash.

Medical & Other

Medical or biohazard waste and sharps, asbestos, creosote railroad ties, and loose food waste.

What can go in: household junk, furniture, remodel and construction debris, roofing shingles, yard waste, and most general material. Fill it right up to the top, just keep everything level with the top rail so we can haul it safely.

Heavy material like concrete, dirt, brick, or roofing is fine, it is just heavy, so give us a heads up first and we will help you load it safely. If the landfill takes it, we can take it. When in doubt, just ask.

Process FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book a dumpster?

Usually same-day or next-day works, especially early in the week. During storm season or a busy stretch, a day or two of notice helps us lock in your spot. Call or text 832-425-7749 and we will find a time.

Where can you place the dumpster?

Most people put it in the driveway, and there you are dealing with your HOA or deed restrictions, not city permitting. We can also set it on the street if your city allows it, which may require a permit first. Rules differ from city to city across our area, so if you need street placement we will check with your city and confirm what is needed. Either way we just need a spot with clear access for the truck and trailer.

Do I need to be home for drop-off or pickup?

No. As long as we know exactly where to place it and the spot is clear, you do not need to be there for drop-off or pickup. A quick text with instructions is plenty.

How do I know when to have it picked up?

Whenever you are done, or when your rental is up, just call or text us and we come get it. If you keep it past the rental you booked, extra days are $100 each.

Can I move the dumpster once it is placed?

Please leave it where we set it. Moving a loaded roll-off yourself can damage your driveway and is not safe. If it needs to move, call us and we will handle it.

What can I put in the roll-off dumpster?

Household junk, old furniture, remodel and construction debris, roofing shingles, yard waste, and most general material. We cannot take hazardous or chemical waste, wet paint or liquids, tires, batteries, Freon appliances, propane, motor oil, or fuel. See the full list of what can't go in, and if you are not sure about something, just ask us before you toss it in.
