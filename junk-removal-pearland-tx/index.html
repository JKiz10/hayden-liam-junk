Two Kinds of House

Shadow Creek One Day, Old Townsite the Next

Pearland is really two housing markets stacked on one city, and a cleanout looks completely different depending on which one you live in.

West of 288 sits the big master-planned stuff. Shadow Creek Ranch and Silverlake are the two everybody names first, with Southern Trails and Silvercreek close behind. Those houses came with two and three car garages and wide concrete aprons out front. Great for storage, which is exactly the problem. A three-car garage out here will quietly absorb years of paint cans, lawn chemicals, outgrown bikes, holiday tubs, and half a bag of concrete before anyone notices the car has been living in the driveway. By the time somebody decides to deal with it, it is never one load.

Then there is the older side, the streets around Old Townsite and out through Green Tee Terrace and West Oaks. Smaller footprints, mature oaks, detached garages set back off a single-width drive, and backyard sheds that have rotted past saving. Different job entirely. More carrying, tighter turns, and usually something structural, a shed or a fence line, that has to come apart before any of it goes on the trailer.

Same phone number, same crew, two completely different plans of attack. Tell us the neighborhood when you call and we already know most of what we are walking into.

Curb Rules

What Your HOA Lets You Leave Out Front

Most of the newer sections of Pearland sit under an association, and those covenants generally have opinions about what can sit at the curb and for how long. We are not going to guess at yours, they all read a little differently, so check your own paperwork. The practical answer lands the same place either way: do not stage it out front at all.

We load from wherever the stuff already is. Garage, side yard, back patio, upstairs bedroom, behind a locked gate if you leave it open for us. Nothing gets dragged to the street first, nothing waits overnight for a truck, and there is nothing sitting out for a neighbor to photograph and forward to the board. A stack of old fence pickets on the grass strip is a violation letter waiting to happen. It is also a quick job for us.
