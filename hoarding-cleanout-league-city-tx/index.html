You Are Not the First Person to Call Us About This

Most people who reach out about a hoarding cleanout spend the first two minutes apologizing. You do not have to do that. This is a category of work we do on purpose, it is not a surprise to us, and there is no version of a house that makes our crew react.

What people are usually afraid of is not the mess. It is being judged, having the neighbors find out, and losing something irreplaceable in the middle of a fast-moving cleanout. Those three things are exactly what our process is built around, and they are written into the scope before anyone touches anything.

Protocol

How Nothing Important Gets Lost

The single most common fear in this work, handled procedurally rather than promised verbally.

  • A written keep list agreed before the first item moves. Specific rooms, specific categories, specific items.
  • Automatic set-aside categories. Documents, photographs, currency, jewelry, firearms, military decorations, and prescription records get pulled and held for you whether or not they were on the list.
  • Nothing questionable leaves without a look. If the crew is unsure, it goes in the review pile, not the truck.
  • A daily review point on multi-day projects, so you check the set-aside before that day's load goes out rather than after.
  • Sealed containers stay sealed until you or your representative opens them.
  • Photo documentation before, during, and at completion, which also protects you if anyone questions what was in the house.

The short version: we do not throw away anything we are not certain about. Cash in a coffee can, a deed in a shoebox, and a wedding ring in a drawer full of junk mail are all things this trade finds routinely. Our job is to make sure they end up in your hands and not in the container.

Boundaries

What We Do and Do Not Take On

Being straight about this up front is worth more to you than a company that says yes to everything and figures it out on day two.

We handle
Severely packed and cluttered properties. Floor-to-ceiling accumulation, goat trails between rooms, decades of storage, blocked exits, and full structural loads of contents. Volume is not a problem. We staff and schedule for it.
We refer out
Human or animal waste, sharps in volume, blood, and heavy active mold. That is licensed biohazard remediation, with different training, equipment, and insurance. Authorization
We need authorization from whoever holds legal authority over the property, whether that is the owner, an executor, a power of attorney, a landlord, or an agency. We confirm this before scheduling, which protects you as much as it protects us.
Occupied properties
Where someone is still living in the home, the pace and the sequence are set by them. We do not force a cleanout. Rushing an occupied hoarding property is how the project collapses halfway through and the family ends up worse off.

Process

How a Cleanout Runs

Private Assessment

We walk the property with you, or with whoever you designate. No photos are taken without permission, nothing is discussed outside the job, and the visit is free. If you would rather meet somewhere else first and walk it later, that is fine too.

Keep List & Written Scope

We build the keep list together, agree the sequence of rooms, and issue one fixed price with a completion date. You know the number and the finish date before anything moves.

Scheduling for Privacy

Start time, truck staging, and container placement are set to draw as little attention as the property allows. If you want it done before the street wakes up, we start before the street wakes up.

Room-by-Room Clearing

We work a defined sequence rather than digging at random, with set-aside review at the end of each day. Steady, contained, and finished on the date in the scope.

Sweep & Handover

Floors swept, set-aside items handed over in person, photo set delivered. The house goes back to you ready for the next step, whether that is cleaning, repairs, listing, or an inspection.

FAQ

Questions People Actually Ask

Will the neighbors know what is happening?

Not from us. We schedule discreetly, including early starts and weekday timing when that draws less attention, we stage the truck and container to be as unobtrusive as the site allows, and the crew does not discuss the job on the property. Privacy is part of the scope, not a favor.

How do you make sure nothing important gets thrown away?

We work against a written keep list agreed before the first item moves, and anything that looks like documents, photographs, currency, jewelry, firearms, or military items is pulled and set aside for you regardless of whether it was on the list. Nothing questionable leaves the property without being shown to you first.

How long does a hoarding cleanout take?

A severely packed single-family home is typically three to five working days, not an afternoon. We staff it accordingly and you get a completion date in the written scope rather than an open-ended estimate.

Do you handle biohazard conditions?

No. Human or animal waste, needles and sharps in volume, blood, and heavy active mold are licensed biohazard remediation work with different training and different insurance. We assess honestly, tell you plainly what we are looking at, and point you to a licensed remediation firm. Once a property is cleared for occupancy we can handle the removal side.

Can a landlord, family member, or agency book the cleanout?

Yes. We regularly work for adult children, property managers, landlords, estate representatives, code enforcement, and social service case workers. We need authorization from whoever has legal authority over the property, and we will confirm that before scheduling.

Do we have to be there while you work?

No, as long as the keep list is agreed and we have access. Plenty of clients cannot be in the house while it happens, and out-of-state family members often hand the whole thing to us and follow along through the daily photo updates.
