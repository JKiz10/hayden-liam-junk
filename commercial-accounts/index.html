FAQ

Commercial Account Questions

What counts toward the 12 dumpsters?

Every dumpster rental your account books, any size and any rental length. A 1-day 14-yard counts the same as a week-long 20-yard.

What happens if we rent fewer than 12 in 6 months?

The 5% and priority junk removal both end, and your account goes back to standard prices and our regular junk removal schedule from then on. We never bill back the 5% on rentals you already had.

How do we get the account price when we book?

Once your account is set up you get your own discount code for online booking, or you can call or text 832-425-7749 and we book it at the account rate.

What does priority junk removal mean?

Account junk removal jobs go to the front of our schedule, and most are done the same day you call. Same day is not guaranteed, and we tell you up front if it cannot happen that day.

Can you send a certificate of insurance?

Yes. H&L is fully insured. We keep a certificate of insurance on file for your account and can name your company or property as additional insured if your policy requires it.

Is there a long contract?

No. It is a one-page account agreement, and either side can close the account at any time by text or email.
