First, About Alvin

An Older Town With Room to Work

Alvin came up on its own rather than as somebody's bedroom subdivision, and it still reads that way. The historic downtown blocks are the actual center of the place, not a themed strip bolted on later. The residential streets ringing them are long settled, with deep lots, grown shade trees, and a garage sitting detached at the far end of the drive instead of hanging off the side of the house. Push a few minutes past the edge of town and the lots stop being lots and start being acreage.

All of that is why the choice on this page is a real choice here, and not in most towns. Two things happen to be true at once in Alvin. There is usually enough driveway to park a roll-off without surrendering your whole approach. And the stuff you want gone is usually nowhere near the street. So both options genuinely work, and picking between them comes down to one thing: whether you want to touch any of it.

Choosing Between Them

Crew or Can? Questions We Get Asked

How do I decide between the crew and the dumpster?

Start with one question: is anybody in the house actually going to carry this stuff? If the answer is no, that settles it and you want the crew. If somebody is willing to load, and the work spreads across more than one day, the roll-off buys you the same result for less. When it is a genuine coin flip, describe the pile to us on the phone and we will tell you which way we would go with it.

Can I use both services on the same Alvin property?

Yes, and on a big cleanout that combination is often the smartest play. The usual version looks like this: the can sits on the driveway for the week while the family works through boxes at whatever speed they can manage, and the crew makes one trip for the things nobody should be lifting, the freezer, the washer, the sectional that will not turn the corner. Book them together and we will line the dates up for you.

Will a roll-off even fit at my place, or should I just have you carry it?

Most Alvin driveways take one without any drama, because lots here are not squeezed the way the newer subdivisions north of town are. What rules it out is soft ground, a low limb hanging over the approach, or the load sitting a long way from anywhere a truck can back to. If the can will not sit right on your property, we will say so before you book and quote the crew instead. Here is how a rental runs start to finish.

What if I book the dumpster and run out of steam halfway through?

It happens, usually somewhere around hour four of a garage. Call us before your term runs out. We can either add days to the rental or send the crew out to load what is left and finish it for you. Just tell us early rather than at pickup, because a can we are already scheduled to grab is much harder to work around. The rental terms spell out the extra-day side of it.

We are closing on the house next week. Which one should we pick?

The crew, nearly every time. A roll-off is a way of buying yourself time, and time is exactly the thing you do not have right now. One visit, the property emptied, the driveway swept, and nobody is working around a can while a buyer, an inspector, or a photographer is trying to park. An estate cleanout runs the same way when a house has to be handed over.

Is one of them cheaper than the other?

It depends entirely on the load, which is why the honest answer has to come after we hear what you have. Our roll-off rates are flat and published on this page, so you can see what a day or a full week costs before you ever call. Junk removal gets quoted against the actual pile, because one sofa and a stuffed garage should not carry the same number. Describe it to us and we will compare the two out loud with you, or read our guide to what moves a haul-off price.
