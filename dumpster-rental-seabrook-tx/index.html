Questions From Seabrook Customers

Can I rent a dumpster in Seabrook for just one day?

Yes, and it is a popular option here. A single day is $365, dropped off and picked up the next morning. If your work lands on a weekend, the $365 weekend special drops Saturday morning and comes back Sunday night. Both include delivery and haul-off.

Will a roll-off fit on a narrow waterfront lot?

Usually, but it is worth checking first. Some Seabrook lots are tight and the approach matters as much as the space itself. Send a photo of where you want it and we will tell you honestly whether the 14-yard works or whether a full-service haul makes more sense.

Can I put dock boards, decking, and fence panels in it?

Yes, those are common Seabrook loads for us. Load them at your own pace and we haul the whole thing off. The exception is chemically treated marine timber, so tell us up front if that is what you are tearing out and we will sort out the right way to handle it.

Do you protect the driveway?

Yes. We place every can on boards to spread the load and protect the surface, and we are fully insured. If the drop spot is pavers or a decorative surface, point it out when you book so we plan the approach.
