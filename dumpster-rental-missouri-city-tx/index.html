Before Anything Else

Where Is It Going to Sit?

Everybody asks about price first. The question that actually decides whether delivery day goes smoothly is where the can lands, and in a suburb this established that is worth two minutes of thought.

Older Fort Bend streets have grown into themselves. Canopy hangs over the curb, over the driveway apron, sometimes over the whole approach. A roll-off coming off the trailer needs vertical clearance, so if there is a low limb across your drive, text us a photo from the street before your date and we will tell you straight whether we clear it or whether the can wants to sit farther out. Nobody enjoys a truck turning around in front of the neighbors.

The surface matters too. Plenty of driveways out here are original concrete with age on them, and plenty of the newer ones are stamped or stained. We board and protect underneath the can either way, and if we have a concern about the spot you picked we say it before the can comes off the trailer, not after it has left a mark.

Read Your Own Paperwork

Sienna, Deed Restrictions, and the Clock

Missouri City covers both ends of the spectrum. On one end you have master-planned Sienna, where the association is active and the paperwork has opinions about what sits in public view and for how long. On the other you have older sections where the rules are looser or effectively unenforced.

We cannot read your deed restrictions for you and we are not going to guess at them, because they vary by section and getting it wrong becomes your letter, not ours. Go look them up. Then tell us the window that keeps you clean and we will build the drop and the pickup around it.

If the window is genuinely short, that is exactly what the two $365 terms exist for. The weekend special is on your drive Saturday morning and off it Sunday night. The single day is shorter still. And if your section will not tolerate a container in view at all, skip the can entirely: our full-service Missouri City junk removal loads onto the trailer and drives away the same visit, so nothing sits out overnight.

What Fills It

Fences, Flooring, and Tired Sheds

The loads we see on this side of the map track the housing stock closely. In the long-settled sections it is replacement work: a fence that finally gave up after years of shifting soil, carpet coming out for LVP, a bathroom stripped to the studs, or a shed at the back of the lot being cut up and carried out board by board.

In Sienna and the newer sections it skews toward upgrades and turnover instead. Cabinet runs pulled during a kitchen refresh, tile and underlayment, packaging and flattened boxes after a closing, and the garage clear-out that follows every move whether you planned one or not.

The can handles all of it. A short list of items has to stay out, and rather than half-remember it here we keep it in one place: check the restricted list before you load. If you are unsure about a specific item, ask us before it goes in rather than after we are strapping it down.

Will a roll-off clear the trees on a Quail Valley street?

Usually, but tell us before delivery day rather than after. If you have a limb hanging low across the approach, text us a photo from the street and we will tell you honestly whether we can get under it or whether the can needs to sit farther out.

How long can a dumpster sit in a Sienna driveway?

That is between you and your association, not us, and the rules vary by section so read your own deed restrictions before you book. What we can do is fit the window. Do I need a permit to put a dumpster in Missouri City?

On your own driveway there is nothing to file, and the driveway is where we set almost every can. Is there a weight limit or a tonnage fee?

No tonnage fee on our 14-yard. Load it right to the top rail, just keep it level with the rim so we can strap it and haul it safely and legally. If you are putting in something unusually dense like concrete, tile, or dirt, call first and we will tell you how to load it so the can does not go over.

Depends entirely on who is doing the lifting. If you have help and time, the roll-off almost always wins on price because you supply the labor. If the house belonged to a parent and you are doing it alone with a closing date coming, full-service junk removal is usually worth the difference.
