Questions

House Clean Out Questions

How much does a house clean out cost?

Every house is different, so we quote each job. Send photos, or have us come walk it with you. You get one written price before anything is loaded, and that is the price. We never charge by the hour on a big job.

Can you come look at the house before you quote?

Yes. For a whole house, seeing it in person helps us get the price right. Call 832-425-7749 or text us and we will set a time to walk it with you. The quote is free, with no obligation.

Can you clear the whole house in one visit?

It depends on the size of the house and how full it is. A big or very full house can take more than one load, or more than one day. We tell you that up front when we quote, and the one written price covers the whole job. Have a move-out or closing date? Tell us the date and we will plan around it.

What if there are things we want to keep?

Tell us before we start, and we set them aside. We work room by room, so you have time to check drawers and closets before anything leaves. Anything you want donated, we are glad to drop at a local charity when we can.

What does a house clean out include?

The contents of the house, room by room: furniture, beds, appliances, boxes, clothes and years of storage, plus the attic, garage and shed. Tell us what stays, and we clear the rest. Not sure about something? Text us a photo and ask.
