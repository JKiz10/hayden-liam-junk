Rentals & Turnovers

Between One Tenant and the Next

A rough turnover has a rhythm to it. The keys come back, the door opens, and there is a couch nobody wanted, a mattress leaned against the wall, bags in the closet, and a kitchen half packed. Nobody on your payroll wants to spend three days carrying that out, and the unit is not earning a dollar while it sits there.

That call is ours. We strip the unit and hand it back ready for paint. Landlords and property managers around Pasadena tend to book us the same way every time, which suits us fine. If you run more than a couple of doors, our property manager page lays out how we handle repeat work.

Shops, Trades & Small Commercial

The Working Side of Town

Plenty of Pasadena runs on shift work, shop bays and trade trucks, and that side of town generates its own kind of pile. A back room stacked with broken shelving. Pallets that came in with a delivery and never left. Office chairs with a busted cylinder. A rack of dead inventory the last owner never dealt with.

We clear all of it, and we can work after close if a loaded trailer in the lot would be in the way during business hours. Contractors call us too. Tear-out from a job on the Fairmont Parkway or Spencer Highway side gets stacked in the driveway and we pull it before the next trade shows up. Straight answer up front: we haul what a crew takes apart, we do not take structures apart ourselves.

If a project is going to run for days and you would rather load at your own pace, ask about a roll-off instead. Same company, same straightforward terms, dropped where you want it and hauled when you are done.

Questions We Get Here

Can you clear a whole rental unit in Pasadena between tenants?

Yes, and it is a good chunk of what we do here. Left-behind couches, bagged clothes, a mattress somebody dragged to the curb, half a kitchen of dishes, the storage closet nobody emptied. We take the unit down to bare floor so your cleaner or painter walks into an empty room. Send a couple of photos to 832-425-7749 and we will price it before we drive out.

Our detached garage in an older part of Pasadena has not been opened in years. Is that too much?

Not even close. Detached garages and backyard sheds on the older Pasadena blocks are our bread and butter. Bikes with flat tires, paint shelves, a dead mower, boxes that got soft on the bottom, an extension ladder nobody uses. You point, we carry it out. We will not tear the building down, but we will empty it completely.

Do you take debris from a Pasadena remodel?

We do. Pulled cabinets, counter slabs, tile, baseboard, rolled carpet and pad, doors, and the packaging that piles up behind the job. Homeowners call us and so do the guys running the work. If your crew has already stacked it in the driveway or the side yard, that is exactly how we like to find it.

We run a small shop off Shaver. Can you clear out the back?

Yes. Small commercial is a normal day for us. Broken shelving, busted pallets, old display racks, dead office chairs, filing cabinets, and whatever has been stacked against the back wall since the last owner. Tell us whether we are working during business hours or after close and we will plan the visit around it.
