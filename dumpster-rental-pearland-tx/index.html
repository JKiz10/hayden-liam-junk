Permits

Driveway, Not the Street

Pearland requires a permit to place a container in the public right-of-way, which means the street or the sidewalk. It does not require one to put a dumpster on your own driveway, and the driveway is where we set nearly every can anyway. So for the overwhelming majority of jobs here, there is no permit and no paperwork.

If your job genuinely needs street placement, say so when you call. We will confirm the current requirement with the City of Pearland before we deliver rather than guess at it, because rules change and a wrong answer becomes your problem, not ours.

One quirk worth knowing: Pearland sits across Brazoria, Harris, and Fort Bend counties. Two neighbors on the same street can technically fall under different county offices. It changes nothing about your rate or our schedule. It only matters if a permit or inspection question comes up, and we will help you work out who to call.
