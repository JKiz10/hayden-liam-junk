Who Calls Us Here

Small Town, Steady Turnover

Kemah covers roughly two square miles and holds around 1,800 residents, yet the city's press kit says it draws more than 3 million visitors a year. It has been a place for second homes for a long time too: the city's history page lists summer homes and fishing camps in town by 1914.

So the calls come from short-term rental owners who need a unit turned, homeowners on the bay clearing a storage room, families sorting out a long-held house, and operators of restaurants and shops around the Lighthouse District.

We run these jobs from our yard in Pearland, about 16 miles away in a straight line and inside the 20-mile circle on the map above. That is not next door, so we say plainly what the schedule allows: same-day or next-day when it has room, a firm window when it does not.

City Pickup

What the Thursday Truck Covers and Where It Stops

Trash service here belongs to AmeriWaste. Per the city's Trash and Recycling page, as of October 2026, heavy trash is collected on Thursdays, curbside. The limit is two items, each under 50 pounds. Appliances, furniture and mattresses all count, and additional fees may apply.

Then the fine print. AmeriWaste crews cannot go onto private property, so everything has to be at the curb by 7 a.m. An appliance with refrigerant has to be drained and tagged by a certified refrigerant recycler first, and a water heater drained before it goes out. Sheetrock, concrete, lumber and bricks count as construction debris, a separate call to AmeriWaste at 281-331-8400.

An AmeriWaste flyer on the city's site, an older document so confirm anything that matters, adds that the city will not provide extra on-call brush or debris pickup and that tires, junk cars, engines and oil are not part of the curbside service. We do not take wet paint, motor oil, gasoline, propane tanks, chemicals or medical waste either, since those need special disposal.

When a crew beats waiting for Thursday. More than two items. A sleeper sofa, a king mattress set or a chest freezer that no one person walks to the curb. Anything upstairs or behind the house, since nobody from the truck will come get it. A pile with drywall or old lumber in it. Any job with a deadline, because Thursday comes once a week.

Rentals

Turning a Short-Term Rental Without Losing a Weekend

The town treats short-term rentals as a business, and the city's owner page spells it out. Under Ordinance 1198 every one has to register, and the city inspects within 30 days of the permit application, checking maximum occupancy, on-site parking and minimum health and safety standards.

None of that is our department, but it explains the rhythm of the work: a mattress that has given up, a sofa with a stain nobody can clean, a dishwasher that died between guests, boxes stacked where a car is supposed to park. The crew goes up the stairs, brings it down and loads it, so what is left for you is the cleaning and the restock.

Tell us when checkout is and when the next guest arrives. If you manage several units, say so. There is more on how we work with property managers.

Bay Houses

Raised Houses, Small Lots and Storm Leftovers

A bay house raised on columns changes the job. The storage is often under the house, the stairs can be steep and the drive short. Since the city's truck cannot come onto the property, someone has to carry everything to the curb first. Our crew does that carrying.

FEMA's profile of one owner who raised his bay house onto concrete columns says it came through Hurricane Ike in 2008 in excellent shape while many neighbors' houses were shattered. After Harvey in 2017, the mayor's letter named Anders Lane and Kemah Oaks as the main places where homes and businesses took floodwater. Wet drywall, carpet, ruined furniture and fence panels are the kind of load that follows.

Small lots mean small staging room. Tell us where the trailer can sit and how many stairs are in the way, and we plan the carry before we arrive.

Yes. Tell us when checkout is and when the next guest arrives, and we will say what the schedule allows. We aim for same-day or next-day but will not promise a slot we cannot keep. We are fully insured. Text photos to 832-425-7749.
