The Difference

Nothing Has to Reach the Curb

Alvin is not laid out like the master-planned subdivisions north of it. Lots run deep. Garages sit detached at the far end of a long driveway. The shed somebody wants emptied is past the garden, behind a fence line, at the back corner of the property. Plenty of outfits will quote that job over the phone and then show up expecting to find it all stacked neatly by the street.

That is not how we work. Where it sits is where we start. If the dead chest freezer is in a back garage and the trailer is parked out front, the walk belongs to us. The Usual Calls
