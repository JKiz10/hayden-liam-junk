By the Job

What Fits in Each Size

The 14-Yard Usually Covers

A garage or attic cleanout, one room of remodel debris, a modest roof, or a fence and yard cleanup. It is the one most homeowners pick.

The 20-Yard Usually Covers

A whole-house clear-out, a kitchen gut, flooring from several rooms, a bigger roof tear-off, or any job where you are already worried about running out of room.

Between Sizes?

Take the 20-yard. Running out halfway through a job costs more than the difference, and the bigger one sits on the same spot. Text us a photo and we will tell you which one fits.

Other Sizes

Looking for a 10, 15 or 30-Yard Dumpster?

We keep two sizes on purpose. If you were shopping for a 10-yard or a 15-yard, our 14-yard is the closest match and the usual pick for a garage cleanout or one room of remodel debris. If you were looking at a bigger can for a bigger job, look at our 20-yard.

We do not rent a 30-yard or a 40-yard. Those are made for commercial lots and tear-downs with room to spare, not a two-car driveway. Both of our sizes are short enough to sit on a normal driveway, on boards, without hanging into the street.

The Price

What Changes the Price, and What Does Not

What Changes It

The size and the rental length (1, 3 or 7 days). The weekend special is a 14-yard rate booked by phone. An extra day is $50, only when the dumpster is not already booked for the next driveway. Cancelling inside 24 hours of a confirmed drop-off keeps $50.

What Does Not

Weight. We do not own a scale, so a heavy load costs the same as a light one. No fuel fee, no delivery fee, and haul-off is included. Fill it level with the top rail and that is the whole rule.

Heavy Loads

Shingles, concrete and dirt reach a safe towing weight before the dumpster looks full, so text us before you load them and we will tell you where to stop. The weight never changes your price.

Some things cannot go in at all: tires, batteries, Freon appliances, chemicals and paint are banned from Texas landfills. See the full list of what cannot go in a dumpster, and the rental terms in plain English.

Straight Answers

Dumpster Size and Price Questions

How much does it cost to rent a dumpster?

The 14-yard is $379.99 for 1 day, $399.99 for the weekend (call to book), $419.99 for 3 days and $549.99 for 7 days. The 20-yard is $499.99 for 1 day, $549.99 for 3 days and $649.99 for 7 days. Delivery, your rental days and haul-off are included, with no weight fee.

What size dumpster do I need for a garage cleanout?

The 14-yard, almost always. A packed two-car garage that also holds furniture from the house can call for the 20-yard. Text us a photo and we will tell you.

What size dumpster do I need for a roof?

A modest roof usually fits the 14-yard and a bigger tear-off calls for the 20-yard. Shingles are heavy, so text us before you load and we will tell you where to stop. The weight never changes your price.

Will a 20-yard dumpster fit in my driveway?

If the 14-yard fits, the 20-yard fits. Both are about 14 ft long and 7 ft wide, and a standard two-car driveway holds either one. We lay boards down before the dumpster touches your concrete.

Can I rent a dumpster for just one day?

Yes. One day is $379.99 for the 14-yard and $499.99 for the 20-yard. Book it online or call or text 832-425-7749.
