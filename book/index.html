No Surprise Bill

We do not own a scale.

Why roll-off bills jump after the dumpster is gone

Most roll-off companies around Houston pay their landfill by the ton, so they have to weigh what you throw away. The price you see online carries a weight allowance, usually about 3 tons, and every ton over it runs $75 to $95. Nobody can weigh a pile of drywall and shingles standing in their own driveway, so you load it, they haul it, and the overage bill shows up after the dumpster is gone.

Why ours can’t

Our landfill bills us by the cubic yard, not by the ton. A full dumpster costs us the same whether it holds couch cushions or roof shingles, so there is no weight cost for us to pass on: no scale, no weigh ticket, and no line for it on your bill. Fill it level with the top rail and the price you booked is the price.

We call it the No-Scale Rate.

Who it helps most, straight up

If you are tossing light stuff, like furniture, boxes, or a garage cleanout, you will rarely hit anyone’s weight allowance, and a company with a lower sticker price may cost you less. The No-Scale Rate pays off on heavy work: roof tear-offs, tile and flooring, broken concrete, and remodel debris. That is where per-ton charges stack up.

Heavy material still has a safe towing limit. Concrete, brick, dirt, rock, and shingles reach it before the dumpster looks full, so call or text before you load them and we will tell you where to stop. That limit is about safety. It does not change your price.

Before You Book: Straight Answers

Is the price on this page really the whole price?

Yes. The 14-yard is $380 for a day, $399 for the weekend, $425 for three days, and $550 for a week. The 20-yard is $520 for a day, $550 for the weekend, $575 for three days, or $675 for a week. Drop-off, your rental days, and haul-off are all inside that number. We do not own a scale, because our landfill bills us by the yard, so there is no weight or tonnage fee. The only other charge is $50 if you cancel inside 24 hours, and it is written out in our rental terms. Need it longer? Contact us to extend.

When will it show up?

Pick your day in the storefront and we text you to confirm the day and drop-off time. Both trailers are kept at our Pearland yard, so same-day and next-day are often open nearby. If your day is already taken, we tell you straight away and work out the closest open day with you.

Will it damage my driveway?

We lay boards down before the dumpster touches your concrete, and we are fully insured. Both sizes take the same spot, about 14 ft long and 7 ft wide, so a standard two-car driveway holds either one.

Which size do I need?

Both sizes take the same 14 by 7 ft spot; the 20-yard just has taller sides. One room of remodel debris or a garage cleanout usually fits the 14-yard. A whole-house clear-out, flooring from several rooms, or a job where you are already worried about running out calls for the 20-yard. If you are between sizes, take the 20: running out halfway costs more than the difference. Text us a photo and we will tell you.

Can I put shingles, concrete, or dirt in it?

Yes, and heavy loads are where the No-Scale Rate saves you the most, because that is what gets hit with per-ton fees elsewhere. Heavy material reaches a safe towing weight before the dumpster looks full, so text us before you load it and we will tell you where to stop. That limit is about safety. It does not change your price.

What if my project runs long?

Call or text us before your pickup day to extend. We can when the dumpster is not already booked for the next driveway, but it often is, so book the rental length the job really needs.

What if I need to cancel or move my date?

Moving your date is free with more than 24 hours of notice. If you cancel inside 24 hours of a confirmed drop-off, $50 is kept and the rest comes back. It is all in our rental terms.
