Garage Clean Out Questions

Do you do garage clean outs near me?

If you are inside the circle on the map, yes. That is about 20 miles around our Pearland yard: Pearland, League City, Friendswood, Manvel, Alvin, Webster, Clear Lake, Dickinson, Kemah, Seabrook, Santa Fe, Missouri City and the south Houston area. We don’t cover inner Houston. Not sure about your street? Text us your address and we’ll tell you straight.

How much does a garage clean out cost?

It depends on how much is in there and what it is, so we quote each job. Text us photos, or for a big garage we can come look for free. You get one written price before anything is loaded, and that is the price. We never charge by the hour on a big job.

Do I have to sort or move anything first?

No. Leave it where it is and tell us what goes and what stays. We do all the carrying and loading. If you want to keep a shelf or a workbench, we work around it.

Will you take an old fridge, a freezer or paint?

Old fridges and freezers, yes, we carry them out (more on our appliance removal page). Paint and household chemicals have special disposal rules, so we’ll point you to the right drop-off for those. Everything else in the garage, we haul.

Are you a garage cleaning or organizing company?

No, and we want to be upfront about that. We’re a junk removal crew. We take out what you don’t want, haul it away and sweep up. We don’t install storage, organize shelves or deep-clean floors. If that’s your plan, we’re a good first step: you start with an empty garage.
