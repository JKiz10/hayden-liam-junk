The Street Rule

Houston Wants a Permit for a Can in the Street

Clear Lake City was annexed into Houston in 1977, so Houston's street code covers its Houston streets. Chapter 40 of the Houston Code of Ordinances, Article XVII, lists a bulk waste container as an obstruction when it sits in a roadway or on a sidewalk, and says no one may place one, or allow one, without a permit. The roadway counts the lane where you park along the curb.

The code excuses the city's own carts and heavy trash set out for the city crew, then says plainly that the excuse does not reach dumpster or roll-off containers.

The permit comes from Houston Public Works, and the city's permit page describes a company-profile process: an account on the city's GeoLink HUB system and an insurance certificate naming the City of Houston, and the code asks you to apply at least ten business days ahead. The fee is charged by the week and, per the code, not prorated for a shorter stay.

Skip it and the city can remove the can and bill whoever put it there, and a violation is a criminal offense with a fine. The permit office line on that page is 832.395.3020.

So we plan on the driveway, and only talk about the street when there is no other spot. If the street is your only option, call or text before you book, and we will check what the city needs before we set a date. Not on a Houston street at all? See which city you are in below.

Read Your Own Paperwork

No Zoning, So the Deed Restrictions Decide

Houston has no zoning, as the city's legal department says on its deed restriction page. What a street tolerates in a driveway comes from restrictions recorded against each subdivision, enforced by an association, by neighbors, or in some cases with the city's help.

A big piece of Clear Lake City sits inside the Clear Lake City Community Association, which says its area runs between Highway 3 and Space Center Boulevard and between Pineloch and NASA Parkway. Its deed restrictions page calls the place an aging community, and its pages name sections like Camino South, Meadowgreen, and Oakbrook West.

Two lines from its materials matter to a cleanout. Exterior changes need an architectural review request approved first, and there should be no storage of clutter or debris in public view. Whether a rental can counts is a question for the association, not for us.

We found no rule about rental dumpsters in what the association posts publicly, which is not the same as permission. Call it at 281-488-0360, or ask the Harris County Clerk for your section's recorded restrictions (713.755.6405, per the city page).

Before You Call a Hauler

The City Truck Takes the Couch, Not the Roof

Houston picks up bulky trash for eligible homes, and it is worth using when your pile fits. Per the city's heavy trash page, collection comes every other month, up to six times a year, on a day assigned to your address. Set the pile just behind the curb between 6 p.m. the Friday before and 7 a.m. on your day, never in the street or on the sidewalk. Furniture, carpet, mattresses, and stoves or washers go.

The not-accepted list is where remodels land: roofing and shingles, construction or demolition debris, dirt, electronics, paint, and chemicals. The city says contractor debris is not collected at all, and a mixed pile may be refused whole. The cap is 8 cubic yards per set-out, and a homeowner's own building material counts up to 4 of those, never shingles, brick, plaster, or concrete.

Set that beside a 14-yard can, sized at 14 cubic yards. One remodel or one roof is more than the city allowance by itself, and parking a torn-out kitchen in the garage until the next pickup month is not a plan. Use the city route for the old sofa and the can for the work. Find your day on the city's lookup tool or the HTX Collects app.

Roll-Off FAQ

Clear Lake Dumpster Questions

Can I put a dumpster in the street in Clear Lake?

Not without a city permit. Clear Lake City is part of Houston, and Chapter 40 of the Houston code counts a bulk waste container as an obstruction in a roadway or on a sidewalk, with no exception for roll-offs. The permit takes an insurance certificate naming the city, and the code asks you to apply at least ten business days ahead. The driveway skips all of that. If yours will not work, call or text 832-425-7749 before you book.

Will Houston pick up my old shingles and remodel debris at the curb?

No. The city heavy trash page lists roofing and shingles, construction or demolition debris, and dirt as not accepted, and says contractor debris is not collected. Pickup is every other month, capped at 8 cubic yards, and a mixed pile can be refused whole. Those are the loads a roll-off is for.

Do Clear Lake City deed restrictions allow a dumpster in the driveway?

That depends on the recorded restrictions for your section. We found no published rule about rental dumpsters on the Clear Lake City Community Association pages, and we will not guess. Houston has no zoning, so these restrictions do the work. Call the association at 281-488-0360 or ask the Harris County Clerk for your section's restrictions.
